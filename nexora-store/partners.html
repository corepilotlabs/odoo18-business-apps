Truth boundary

The partner program does not claim a product is available before it is actually published, QA-passed and payment-ready. Planned products can be shown as planned, but cannot be ordered or sent to Factory as a white-label build.

  • No Saudi outbound partner prospecting.
  • No Factory build before verified partner + verified PayPal payment.
  • No public merchant email or PayPal credentials in the Store.
  • No branded delivery before dedicated white-label QA passes.
