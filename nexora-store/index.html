Factory rules

Build gate

  • At least three independent business signals.
  • At least two independent evidence references.
  • Problem severity and demand score must pass the threshold.
  • Saudi evidence is excluded from outbound-target scoring.

Publish gate

  • QA must pass.
  • A verifiable release must exist.
  • A working demo must exist.
  • Problem evidence remains attached to the product record.

International-only outbound

NEXORA Store V1 explicitly blocks Saudi Arabia from outbound prospecting and marketing. Unknown country records are also blocked until geography is verified.

PayPal checkout

Paid products use secure PayPal Payment Links only after the product is published. The merchant email is not exposed in the public storefront. Revenue is counted only after verified payment evidence.
