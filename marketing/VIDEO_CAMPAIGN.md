# Video 3 — Purchase Price Control

**Target duration:** 60–75 seconds

### Hook
"The purchase order can be valid — and still be too expensive."

### Problem
A buyer may approve a higher vendor price without seeing the previous price, historical average, best known price or a better supplier alternative.

### Mechanism
**Current price → Previous vendor price → Historical average → Best historical price → Alternative vendor → Variance → Risk → Approval decision**

### Functions to show
- Previous vendor price comparison
- Vendor historical average
- Best historical price
- Alternative-vendor comparison
- Price variance visibility
- Risk-oriented alerts
- Potential savings visibility
- Vendor intelligence
- Controlled approval for significant increases

### CTA
"Put price history in front of the decision-maker before approval. Available on Odoo 18 and Odoo 19."

# Video 4 — Smart Transaction Capture

**Target duration:** 60–75 seconds

### Hook
"OCR is fast. Unchecked OCR can also automate mistakes."

### Problem
Invoice, PDF and scanned-image entry is repetitive. Pure extraction does not validate the accounting result.

### Mechanism
**Document → OCR/extraction → Partner match → Account/tax match → Confidence → Duplicate check → Totals validation → Review → Controlled draft creation → Verification**

### Functions to show
- PDF and scanned-image capture
- OCR-assisted extraction
- Supplier / partner matching
- Account and tax matching support
- Confidence scoring
- Duplicate invoice detection
- Subtotal / tax / total validation
- Review of inconsistent evidence
- Controlled draft creation
- Post-creation traceability

### CTA
"Faster capture without losing accounting control. Available on Odoo 18 and Odoo 19."

# LinkedIn publishing pattern

Use the 60-second overview first. Then publish the three focused product videos separately rather than releasing four videos at once.

For every post:
1. Open with the business problem, not the product name.
2. Show the mechanism visually.
3. Show 3–5 high-value functions only.
4. End with Odoo 18 + Odoo 19 availability.
5. Point to the public GitHub showroom and Odoo Apps Store listings.
