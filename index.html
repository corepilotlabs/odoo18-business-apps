DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Automation with boundaries.

For high-impact business operations, speed without control can magnify the original error. CorePilot products use a stronger pattern: evidence before action, explicit checkpoints where needed, and verification afterward.

01Fail closed when evidence is not good enough.Uncertainty should stop sensitive execution, not be hidden.
02Separate diagnosis from execution.Understand the cause before changing data.
03Keep traceability.Record what was checked, approved and verified.
