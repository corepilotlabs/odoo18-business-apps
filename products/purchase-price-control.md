## The problem

A purchase order can be technically valid and still be commercially poor. Buyers may approve a higher price without seeing the previous vendor price, historical average, best historical price or a better alternative supplier.

## What it does

Purchase Price Control adds price intelligence and approval control to Odoo 19 purchasing. It compares the current RFQ / purchase order price against relevant history and supplier alternatives, surfaces variance and risk, records price history and highlights potential savings before high-price orders are approved.

## Core capabilities

- Previous vendor price comparison
- Vendor historical average
- Best historical price
- Alternative-vendor comparison
- Price variance alerts and risk levels
- Potential savings
- Normalized currency and unit-of-measure comparison
- Vendor intelligence
- Controlled approval for high-price purchase orders
