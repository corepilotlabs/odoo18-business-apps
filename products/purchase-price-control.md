## The problem

A purchase order can be technically valid and still be commercially poor. Buyers may approve a higher price without seeing the previous vendor price, historical average, best known price or a better supplier alternative.

## What it does

Purchase Price Control adds price intelligence and approval context to **Odoo 18 purchasing**. It helps users detect meaningful price increases before approval instead of discovering them after the purchase has already been committed.

## Core capabilities

- Previous vendor price comparison
- Vendor historical average
- Best historical price
- Alternative-vendor comparison
- Price variance visibility
- Risk-oriented alerts
- Potential savings visibility
- Vendor intelligence
- Controlled approval for significant price increases

## Why it matters

The value is not another purchase report. The value is putting the right historical context in front of the person making the decision, at the time the decision is made.

## Best fit

Procurement teams, finance controllers and businesses that want stronger control over purchase-price increases without forcing users to search manually across old purchase orders.
